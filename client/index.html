Benefits of Blood Donation

For the Donor

  • Free Health Check-up: Before donating, your pulse, blood pressure, body temperature, and hemoglobin levels are checked. Your blood is also screened for several diseases.
  • Supports Heart Health: Regular donation can help reduce blood viscosity, which may improve blood flow and lower the risk of heart disease.
  • Calorie Burn: The body uses energy to replace the donated blood, burning approximately 650 calories per donation session.
  • Emotional Well-being: Knowing that a single donation can save up to three lives provides a significant sense of pride and community connection.

For the Recipients

  • Emergency Surgeries: Blood is essential for patients undergoing major surgeries or those involved in serious accidents.
  • Cancer Treatment: Many cancer patients require blood or platelet transfusions during chemotherapy.
  • Chronic Illness Management: People with conditions like Thalassemia or Sickle Cell Anemia rely on regular transfusions to survive and stay healthy.
  • Childbirth Complications: Blood supplies are critical for mothers experiencing complications during or after delivery.
